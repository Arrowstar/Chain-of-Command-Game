# Fumble Deck — AI Art Prompts

**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (1520×855 or 760×428)
**Style:** Tactical sci-fi, semi-realistic digital art, dark moody lighting, holo-cyan UI accents, military spacecraft interior. Consistent with existing game art.

---

## General Fumbles

### `freeze` — "I Can't Think!"
A stressed bridge officer frozen mid-action, hands hovering frozen over a flickering holographic console. Glitching red and cyan display panels cast harsh light across their face. Alarms flashing in the background. Other crew members blurred out of focus behind them. Tight over-the-shoulder shot focused on the officer's hands and the chaotic console.

### `insubordination` — "Shut Up and Let Me Work!"
A bridge officer turned away from their console, shouting angrily at the camera / captain position. Tense standoff on the bridge. Other officers in the background look away uncomfortably. Harsh overhead lighting, long shadows. The officer's hands are clenched. Console displays flickering amber warnings.

### `nerve_collapse` — "Nerve Collapse"
Close-up of a shattered console monitor with a fist driven through it. Sparks showering upward, casting dramatic contrast. An officer's arm visible, uniform sleeve torn. Emergency red lighting pulses across the scene. Broken glass and debris floating in low gravity. Smoke rising from shorted circuits.

### `bickering` — "Bickering / Blame Game"
Two bridge officers face-to-face in heated argument, pointing fingers at each other. Red alert lights strobing across the scene. Console screens behind them show critical system warnings. A third officer in the background tries to mediate, arms raised. Claustrophobic bridge interior, tension visible in body language.

## Helm Fumbles

### `overcompensated_burn` — "Overcompensated Burn"
Side view of a starship lurching violently to starboard, a massive orange engine flare scorching from its thrusters at an awkward angle. Stars streaking past the viewport — the ship is spinning. Interior view shows a helm officer thrown sideways in their chair, reaching for controls. Warning indicators flashing across the helm console.

### `dampener_failure` — "Inertial Dampener Failure"
Interior bridge shot — crew members thrown across the deck by sudden G-forces. Papers, data slates, and a coffee mug floating in zero-G. Red emergency lights. One officer gripping a railing, feet off the ground. A console screen shows a large "INERTIAL DAMPENER OFFLINE" warning in red text. Chaotic, mid-catastrophe moment.

### `nav_lockout` — "Navigational Lockout"
Close-up of a helm navigation display frozen on a star map with a large red "NAV LOCKOUT — SYSTEM HALT" overlay. The officer's hand visible pressing against the unresponsive screen in frustration. Reflections on the glass. Dim emergency lighting, cyan and red display glow illuminating the officer's face from below.

## Tactical Fumbles

### `panic_fire` — "Panic Fire"
A ship-mounted cannon firing at a severe oblique angle, its muzzle flash illuminating an allied ship hull that's dangerously close to the line of fire. Interior inset shows a tactical officer's hand slamming the fire button prematurely. Warning text "FRIENDLY ARC WARNING" flashing on a nearby display. Dramatic orange flash, debris in the beam path.

### `misfire` — "Misfire / Power Surge"
A weapon turret on a starship hull arcs with electrical surges — blue-white lightning crawling across the barrel. Smoke venting from a scorched panel. Interior view shows a tactical console sparking, the officer recoiling. Warning symbols on the HUD. Dark exterior space backdrop with the weapon silhouette catching the electrical glow.

### `tunnel_vision` — "Tunnel Vision"
A targeting reticle zoomed in extremely tight on an enemy ship, the rest of the viewport blurred and darkened. HUD elements around the edges show other threats highlighted in red with small "PDC OFFLINE" warning icons. The tactical officer's face illuminated only by the targeting screen. Cinematic vignette effect.

### `ordnance_jam` — "Ordnance Jam"
Close-up of a torpedo tube with a missile visibly stuck halfway out, warning lights reflecting off its casing. Red caution stripes around the tube opening. A technician in the foreground holding a diagnostic tool. Holographic schematic overlay showing the jam with red highlighted fault lines. Smoke or venting gas.

## Engineering Fumbles

### `catastrophic_reroute` — "Catastrophic Reroute"
A holographic shield grid diagram showing one sector collapsing — represented as red hexagonal cells dissolving while the others remain blue. An engineer's hand mid-reach toward the hologram, too late. Sparks flying from a nearby panel. Contrast between the cold blue shields and the angry red failure zone.

### `coolant_vent` — "Coolant Vent"
A corridor filled with glowing green toxic coolant mist, knee-deep and rising. Emergency lights casting long shadows through the fog. Warning strips on the walls. A figure in an environmental suit visible further down the corridor, moving toward a leak source. Industrial pipes and valves in a cramped engineering passageway.

### `false_diagnostics` — "False Diagnostics"
An engineering officer staring intently at one display screen while a different screen behind them shows a critical alert going unnoticed. Tangled cables crossing between consoles. The officer's face shows confusion and stress. Ambient amber lighting. The contrast between the screen they're reading and the ignored warning creates narrative tension.

## Sensors Fumbles

### `ghost_contacts` — "Ghost Contacts"
A circular radar/sensor display showing dozens of false contact signatures — blinking red dots scattered everywhere. Too many to be real. A sensor officer's hand hovering over the console in confusion. The display casts cyan light on their face. Wires dangling from an open access panel nearby. Visual clutter, information overload.

### `comms_blackout` — "Comms Blackout"
A communications array dish on the ship's exterior, severed cable sparking and floating in zero-G. The broken end of a thick data cable drifts in space, illuminated by intermittent electrical arcs. Through a nearby viewport, an officer can be seen speaking into a headset with a frustrated expression.

### `targeting_feedback_loop` — "Targeting Feedback Loop"
A sensor dish or radar array on the hull glowing white-hot from overload, concentric energy rings radiating outward like a screaming broadcast. The energy visible as pulsing wave rings in space. Interior shows a sensor officer covering their ears, displays flickering with distorted waveforms. Intense, painful-looking visual feedback.
